# 🪢 JS-FASTROPE
### Military Fast-Rope for FiveM

**Helicopter ropes · Controlled descent · Skill that grows with practice**

🎬 [**Watch the showcase**](https://youtu.be/vMiGMFlGVxA) · 📥 [**Free download on Discord**](https://discord.gg/XEpk5MUJEh)

**🆓 100% FREE**

The pilot taps **X** — the ropes drop from both doors to the ground. Operators press **E** and slide down.
Loosen your grip to drop fast, squeeze to brake a few meters above the ground… or hit it too fast and fall.
Your landing depends on **your speed, your gear and your skill**.

## ✨ Features
- 🚁 **Ropes from both doors** — Valkyrie, Annihilator, Cargobob, Buzzard + add-on helicopters
- 🧭 **Pilot panel** — rope status, height, *"reaches the ground"* or *"short by X m"*, speed, operators on the rope
- ✂️ **Emergency rope cut** (hold X) · ropes tear above 40 kt
- 🧤 **Gloves required** — braking heats them up, grip strength runs out
- 🌬️ **Swinging rope**, end of the rope, operators queue on the rope
- 🟢🟠🔴 **Speed HUD** — green safe · orange brake soon · red **BRAKE!**
- 🤕 **Falls and injuries** — helmet and knee pads reduce them
- 🎖️ **Skill system** — Trainee → Qualified → Experienced → Instructor (saved in SQL)
- 🛡️ **Server-side check** — every landing is computed by the server, no fake perfect landings
- ⚡ **Optimized** — near-zero load outside a helicopter

## 🎮 Controls
| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `X` (tap) | Ropes out / in *(pilot)* |
| `X` (hold) | Emergency cut *(pilot)* |
| `E` | Go down the rope |
| `W` | Loosen grip — slide fast |
| `S` | Squeeze — brake |
| `Space` | Let go of the rope |

## 💬 Commands
| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/fastrope` | Your skill card |
| `/fastropeadmin <id> <0-100 \| reset>` | Set / reset a player's skill *(admin)* |

## 📦 Requirements
- QBCore / Qbox / ESX / standalone
- ox / qs / qb / ps / lj inventory
- oxmysql *(optional — without it, skills are saved to a file)*
- Works with or without OneSync
- English + Hebrew included
